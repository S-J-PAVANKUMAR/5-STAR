Budget Saving Tips

Book Early

Early booking helps you get lower prices on flights and hotels.

Use Public Transport

Buses and trains are cheaper than taxis and rental cars.

Travel Off Season

Traveling during off-season reduces costs and avoids crowds.

Eat Local Food

Local restaurants are affordable and offer authentic food experiences.
