Smart Packing Strategies

Efficient packing helps travelers stay organized, avoid excess baggage charges, and carry everything needed.

Essential Packing Checklist

  • Weather-appropriate clothing
  • Travel documents
  • Chargers and electronics
  • Medicines
  • Toiletries
  • Emergency cash
