Frequently Asked Questions

When should I book flights?

Booking flights 1–2 months early usually gives better prices and more options.

How can I travel safely?

Keep important documents secure, share your location with family, and stay updated on local rules.

What should I pack?

Carry essential clothing, medicines, chargers, travel documents, and emergency contacts.
